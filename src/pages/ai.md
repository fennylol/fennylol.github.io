## What I believe
---
In short, my views on Generative Artificial Intelligence is that it is a tool that provides ***products*** faster by eliminating (or greatly compressing) the ***process***.
In situations where learning from or being shaped by the process is beneficial, I believe that task is best performed by hand.
Otherwise, GenAI can shrink loops, ship features, and ideate on projects faster than tahn we ever have before.


## When I avoid Generative Artificial Intelligence
---
### 1. When the task is one I want to learn
for example: [OneTruePingus].

### 2. When I want to call the work mine
there are times when you want it to be your name on the box

### 3. When I am creating out of passion
art is art

### 4. When I am expressing myself
see above.

## When I use Generative Artificial Intelligence
--- 
### 1. As a debugging companion
dude come on its good at debugging
### 2. As a development companion
when i want it done. or want to see the right way to do it.
### 3. As a problem solver
just recently had it translate the commands stored in old command blocks in an old minecraft world and updated to modern syntax.
no tool exists to rewrite over 600 commands, wouldve taken weeks redoing work.
