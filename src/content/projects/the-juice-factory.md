The juice is ready to go! Fill bottles and mix colors in the right
proportions to succeed. Each truck driver only accepts a certain color, and
more are always pulling up to collect their share — plan ahead, because
demand doesn't let up.

There are no preset levels: keep going until you can't fill a truck in
time. The more the operation scales up, the harder it gets to keep pace.

**Controls:** right-click and drag to pan, scroll to zoom, left-click to
place conveyors and machines (click-and-drag for longer runs) — choose
carefully, because once something's placed, it's staying there.

Built with PolytopeParty in Godot 4.0 for the 2024 GMTK Game Jam, with pixel
art in Aseprite and Procreate.
