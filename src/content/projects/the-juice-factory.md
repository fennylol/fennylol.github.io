Place conveyors and machines to fill bottles with correctly mixed colors for
arriving truck drivers. There are no preset levels — you keep going until you
miss a truck's deadline, and it gets harder as the operation grows.

Built with PolytopeParty in Godot 4 for the 2024 GMTK Game Jam, with pixel art
in Aseprite and Procreate.
