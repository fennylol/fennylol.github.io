*"The juice is ready to go! Fill bottles and mix colors in the right proportions to succeed.*
*Each truck driver only accepts a certain color, and more are always pulling up to collect their share — plan ahead, because demand doesn't let up."*

> ![The Juice Factory Main Menu](/images/the_juice_factory/splash.png "The Juice Factory Main Menu")
> The Juice Factory Main Menu

The Juice Factory is a brutally challenging, rogue-like factory builder.
Delivery trucks need the right color of juice before they miss their delivery.
Bottles must be filled at primary-color faucets, combined in mixing machines, and routed to their waiting trucks.

> ![A Delivery Truck Being Filled](/images/the_juice_factory/gameplay_0.png "A Delivery Truck Being Filled")
> A Delivery Truck Being Filled

As the business grows, new trucks arrive with increasingly complicated orders.
Bottle generators, conveyor belts, mixing machines, and route changers have to be placed so the right bottles reach the right truck in time.

> ![Gameplay Getting More Intense](/images/the_juice_factory/gameplay_1.png "Gameplay Getting More Intense")
> Gameplay Getting More Intense

The theme for the game jam was "Built to Scale".
Machinery and conveyor belts cannot be removed or replaced once installed.
The way your factory is built dictates how well it can scale.

> ![A Delivery Truck About to Miss its Shipment (due to a poorly constructed factory)](/images/the_juice_factory/gameplay_2.png "A Delivery Truck About to Miss its Shipment (due to a poorly constructed factory)")
> A Delivery Truck About to Miss its Shipment (due to a poorly constructed factory)

There are no preset levels: keep going until you can't fill a truck in time.
The more the operation scales up, the harder it gets to keep pace.

Playable in browser.
Made together with [Polytope Party](http://polytope.party/) for the 2024 GMTK game jam and our first ever jam submission.
Built in 72 hours using Godot 4.0, with assets made in Blender, Aseprite, and Procreate.
