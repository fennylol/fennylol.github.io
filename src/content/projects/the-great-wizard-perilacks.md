You are the Great Wizard Perilacks (pronounced *PAIR-uh-LAX*) and you wish to
build yourself a Tower. Use your magic spells to move, place blocks, and
interact through strange and esoteric perspectives. In the end, only the
grandest of towers will be enough!

There are 10 levels that get harder as you go. Collect the coin in each
level for extra accolades — 100% the game by collecting all 10.

**Controls:** keyboard or controller. Press Esc any time to pause and view
the controls.

> Graphics are prettier and controls are snappier in the downloadable
> version — shaders broke entirely in the HTML build. Download the .exe for
> the intended experience.

Built with PolytopeParty in Godot 4.6 for the 2026 Bigmode Game Jam, with
assets made in Blender, Aseprite, and Procreate.
