The [Mano Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mano_machine) is a 16-bit general-purpose CPU designed and described by M. Morris Mano.
As an academic exercise, guided by a course instructor, we followed his work and replicated it long-hand.
We reasoned through and designed an instruction set, constructed truth tables and Karnaugh maps, derived minimal boolean expressions, and finally mapped those expressions to combinational logic.
Lastly we assembled the working CPU in a logic simulator: the instruction decoder, control logic, ALU, and memory bus.
