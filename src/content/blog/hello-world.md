Every personal site needs a first post that nobody reads. This is that post.

## Why this exists

I wanted one place that holds my work — projects, writing, links to people I
like. Fast to load, simple to maintain, mine.

## What's here

- **Projects** — things I've built.
- **Blog** — notes and write-ups (this).
- **Links** — a blogroll in the old spirit.
- **Now** — what I'm up to lately.

## Colophon

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build), hand-written CSS, and no client-side
JavaScript. Hosted as static files. Delete this post whenever.
