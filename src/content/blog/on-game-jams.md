## Why Make Games?
I would not hesitate to call Minecraft the most influential piece of software on my life. 
I played with friends. I played with my brother. I played by myself. I played when I was supposed to be doing homework. I played when I was supposed to be asleep.
How could I stop? It was a digital box of Legos, but the box did not have to be sifted through to find a specific piece. Special blocks were not locked behind catalog pages and Christmas lists. The only limit was what you could put your mind to and what you could manage to achieve before your imagination demanded you table one project for a newer, more ambitious one.
